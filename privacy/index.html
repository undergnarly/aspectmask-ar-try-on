Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ASPECT ("we," "us," "our"), the maker of handmade designer masks and headpieces sold at aspectmask.com (the "Site"), collects, uses, and shares information when you visit the Site.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide. The Site has no accounts or on-site checkout, so it does not collect names, addresses, or payment details directly. If you order through Instagram Direct or WhatsApp, information shared there is also subject to that platform’s privacy policy.

Information collected automatically. The Site may collect pages and product cards viewed, general device/browser type, approximate location derived from IP address, and interactions such as slides viewed or buttons tapped.

2. Cookies & Similar Technologies

The Site uses cookies and similar technologies placed by the services below to understand how the Site is used. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings.

3. Analytics & Advertising

  • Google Analytics (GA4) helps us understand traffic and interactions. See Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • Meta Pixel helps measure Instagram/Facebook traffic and campaigns. See Meta’s Privacy Policy.

4. How We Use Information

  • To improve products, photos, and site sections.
  • To measure organic content and advertising performance.
  • To communicate with you and arrange an order after you contact us.

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared only with the named analytics providers or as needed to fulfil an order, such as with a shipping carrier.

6. Orders, Payment & Shipping

The Site does not process payments or store payment-card information. Orders are arranged individually via Instagram Direct or WhatsApp.

7. Your Choices & Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to processing of your personal data. You can also use the Google Analytics opt-out and manage Meta ad preferences.

8. Children’s Privacy

The Site is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16.

9. International Visitors

The Site is available worldwide and information may be processed in countries other than your own by the providers listed above.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the Site or applicable practices change. The effective date above shows the latest revision.

11. Contact Us

For privacy questions, message @aspect_mask on Instagram.
