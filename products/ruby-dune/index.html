About the artist

Not manufactured.
Authored.

ASPECT is built around a simple idea: a mask should not hide you. It should reveal a version of you that people cannot ignore.

Every piece is sculpted by hand over several days. The flexible structure, wrapped details and final silhouette are formed individually — without moulds, mass production or repeats.

That is why each mask has its own character and its own price. You are not buying an accessory from a production line. You are claiming the only existing version of an artist’s idea.
